Proposed phone Account reference correction

Keep the existing Account entry point and add two routes to existing screens. This changes only the phone Account navigation card in Light and Dark.

Briefings and reports

Choose a journey to open its briefing, updates or reports.

Routes

Open updates → existing dated Updates selector, including journeys without changes.

Open reports → existing Reports chooser/history. Reporting a problem remains a separate action in a briefing.

Access and reflow

Show only destinations allowed by the current account. Where Reports permission is known but connectivity is unavailable, disable its action and show “Connect to open reports.” Cold-offline recovery does not infer Reports permission from a saved briefing.

Stack controls, grow the card and scroll the page at 200% text. Keep Sign out reachable.

The Coordinator recommends this bounded correction. Existing phone references currently leave Reports history and the Updates selector without reliable entry points. Figma and Flutter navigation have not changed yet.
